Privacy Policy for Locarby

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Locarby ("the app", "we", or "our") is a mobile app that helps you find gyms, cafes, restaurants, and other places near an address you choose. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app uses, how it is processed, and the choices available to you.

What We Collect and How We Use It

Location and addresses. To run a search, the app uses Apple's location services built into iOS to convert an address you enter into map coordinates. When you tap "Use current location", your current location is used to look up its street address. The resulting coordinates, search radius, and selected place types are then sent to the Google Places API to find places. These requests go directly from your device to Apple and Google. Our statistics server does not receive your exact location or the address text you entered.

Recent addresses and settings. The last five addresses you search are saved on your device only, using iOS local app storage (UserDefaults). Your app settings, including the light/dark theme and your search-statistics preference, are stored locally as well. This locally stored data is not sent to our server or to third parties as stored data, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Search statistics (optional). The first time you run a search, the app asks whether you want to share search statistics. Only if you choose Allow does the app send the following information to a server we operate:

  • The approximate area searched, represented by a grid cell of about 1 km. The exact coordinates and address text are not sent to our statistics server.
  • The types of places you searched for.
  • The time of the request.

Our server aggregates these requests into daily counters by grid cell and place type (for example, the number of searches for cafes in a particular area on a given day). We use these counters to understand where people search for places. The statistics are not linked to your name, a user account, or a device identity.

You can change your choice at any time using Share search statistics at the bottom of the search screen. When this setting is off, the app sends no new search statistics to our server. Turning it off does not automatically delete statistics that have already been aggregated.

App verification and abuse prevention. To help verify that statistics requests come from a legitimate Locarby app installation, the app uses Apple's App Attest service. When statistics are first sent, the app creates a random key for that installation. Our server stores the public part of the key and the time of its last request to verify requests and limit them to one per second. This key is not combined with the search statistics.

When a request reaches our server, the server also receives its IP address. We use the IP address only temporarily, in memory, to limit abuse. We do not store or log it.

Retention of statistics. Aggregated search counters are retained for 12 months and then automatically deleted. An installation's App Attest public key is also deleted if it has not sent a request for 12 months. In areas with very few searches, aggregated statistics may reflect activity from only a small number of people. We keep these statistics private and do not publish them.

We process optional search statistics based on your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by turning off the search-statistics setting.

Other personal information. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, or photos. The app does not have user accounts or sign-in, and it does not use advertising SDKs or tracking identifiers.

Third-Party Services

Google Places API. Locarby uses the Google Places API to search for and display places, ratings, and, where available, price or fuel information. When you run a search, your chosen location, search radius, and selected place types are sent to Google to fulfil the request. Google's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy .

Apple location services. Locarby uses Apple's location services built into iOS to convert entered addresses into coordinates and to look up an address for your current location. Apple's handling of data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy .

Maps apps. If you tap "Open in Apple Maps" or "Open in Google Maps", the selected place's location is passed to the relevant Maps app. The receiving app handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our hosting provider. The optional search statistics described above are sent to a server we operate using a hosting provider. The provider processes information as needed to host and operate that server on our behalf.

Your Choices

  • Location access: You can change or revoke location permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Locarby. You can still search by entering an address manually.
  • Precise Location: You can disable Precise Location in the same settings screen. Locarby will use the approximate location provided by iOS instead.
  • Recent addresses: On the search location screen, swipe left on an address to remove it, or tap "Clear" at the top of the Recent list to remove all recent addresses.
  • Search statistics: You decide whether to share statistics when the app first asks. You can change this choice at any time using "Share search statistics" at the bottom of the search screen.

Children's Privacy

Locarby is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. If a change materially affects how we process personal data, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by applicable law.
