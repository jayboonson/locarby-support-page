Locarby Support

Help for the Locarby iPhone app.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback? Email zawrao@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, if possible, what you searched for.

Common questions

"Use current location" doesn't work

Check that location access is allowed: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Locarby → While Using the App. You can also search without location by entering an address.

No places are found

Try a larger radius (up to 15 km) or select more categories. Each category shows up to 20 places closest to the address you chose.

How do I remove my recent addresses?

Open the search location screen, swipe left on an address to remove it, or tap "Clear" at the top of the Recent list to remove all of them.

How do I change my search-statistics choice?

Use "Share search statistics" at the bottom of the search screen. When it is off, the app sends no statistics.

Where does the place information come from?

Places, ratings, prices and fuel prices come from Google. If something about a place is wrong, open it in Google Maps from the place's page and suggest an edit there.
